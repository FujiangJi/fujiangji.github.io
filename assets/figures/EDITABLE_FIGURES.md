# Research themes and approaches

This is the earlier diagram version, retained as an editable archive. The current homepage uses single representative illustrations without arrows, plots, labels or enlargement controls. Their original PNGs, compact JPGs and prompts are in `../images/research_areas/representative/`.

`Fujiang_Ji_Research_Themes_and_Approaches.pptx` contains six editable conceptual figures, in homepage order:

1. Plant Functional Traits & Diversity
2. Forest Fragmentation & Ecosystem Function
3. Dryland Biocrusts & Climate Feedbacks
4. Imaging Spectroscopy & Multiscale Remote Sensing
5. Data Fusion & Machine Learning
6. Radiative Transfer & Process-based Modeling

Created on 7 October 2026 using the nature-image2ppt workflow. Original concept illustrations were generated with the built-in image generation tool and reconstructed as separate PowerPoint objects. These figures illustrate research questions and methods; plots, spectra and map cells are conceptual and do not represent measured results.

Text, ordinary arrows, model boxes, plot curves, markers, map cells and neural-network nodes/relations are native editable PowerPoint objects. Complex botanical, soil, landscape and instrument illustrations remain separate transparent bitmap parts that can be moved, resized or replaced. The dense water-infiltration illustration is also a replaceable bitmap part. The data-fusion figure is entirely native.

The six earlier JPGs in `../images/research_areas/` are compact exports of the validated PowerPoint render. Open the PowerPoint to edit that archived artwork. These diagram exports are no longer used by the homepage.
