# Academic institution marks

- `UW_crest.svg`: unmodified full-color academic W Crest from the [UW–Madison digital logo bundle](https://brand.wisc.edu/resource/uw-institutional-logos-for-web-digital-use/). [Official logo guide](https://brand.wisc.edu/visual-identity/logos/).
- `UA_academic.png` and `UA_academic_reverse.png`: unmodified full-color vertical university logos, with the full institutional name, from the [University of Arizona master-logo downloads](https://phoenixmed.arizona.edu/brand-toolkit/logos/master-logo). The reverse version is shown in dark mode.
- Arizona's presidential seal is not used. Its [official guidelines](https://marcom.arizona.edu/resources/presidential-assets) restrict use on websites.

The earlier Block A and athletics W files remain in the asset archive; the academic-history sections use the above replacements.
