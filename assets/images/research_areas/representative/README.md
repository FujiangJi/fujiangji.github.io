# Representative research images

Six single representative illustrations for the homepage, generated with the built-in image generation tool on 7 October 2026. These are conceptual illustrations, not photographs of a specific field site, spacecraft mission or specimen. They contain no explanatory arrows, statistical plots, labels or flow diagrams.

The original PNGs are retained for later image edits. The same-named 640-pixel JPGs are compact website exports. Full generation prompts and the botanical correction prompt are saved in `prompts.json`.

- `plant-traits`: separate broad-leaf and conifer foliage specimens.
- `forest-fragmentation`: forest edges formed by a road and agricultural clearing.
- `biocrust-feedbacks`: moss, lichen and dark biological soil crust.
- `imaging-spectroscopy`: Earth-observing satellite over a landscape.
- `data-fusion`: a scientific workstation displaying aerial ecological imagery.
- `process-modeling`: a sunlit leaf and its internal tissue structure.

The homepage images are static and do not open an enlargement dialog. Existing research and paper links remain in each card.
