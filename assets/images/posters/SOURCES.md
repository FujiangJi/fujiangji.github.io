# Conference poster assets

Five posters supplied by Fujiang Ji, imported on 2026-10-08. Original PDFs are copied byte-for-byte into `assets/posters/`. Poster artwork, wording, and colors are preserved.

Each poster has a 960 px WebP thumbnail, a 2000 px preview, and an 8000 px full-resolution lossless WebP rendering. Only thumbnails load in the Publications gallery. The active poster loads its preview and full image when the reader opens. PDFs are download links and are not loaded by the gallery.

Titles, venue labels, and years are matched to the poster text and existing conference entries in `pages/publications.html`. The PLSR poster is the February 2024 Bryson Scholarship Poster Session, not an AGU poster.

Metadata and asset dimensions: `assets/data/posters.json`.

Rebuild with a Python environment containing Pillow and with Poppler on PATH:

```sh
python3 scripts/build_poster_images.py
```
